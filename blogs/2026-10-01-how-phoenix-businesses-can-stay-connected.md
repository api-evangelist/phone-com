Phoenix is becoming more than a fast-growing city. It is developing into a major business hub where technology, advanced manufacturing, healthcare innovation, and professional services increasingly overlap. The City of Phoenix Economic Development and Education Strategic Plan identifies emerging technology, manufacturing, producer services, and bioscience among the city’s key economic priorities.
