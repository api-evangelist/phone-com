Cryptography phones—more commonly called secure or encrypted phones—are the latest chapter in a much older effort to keep information private. Long before smartphones, people were disguising military orders, diplomatic messages, and trade secrets so only the intended recipient could understand them. What changed over time was not the basic goal, but the technology.
